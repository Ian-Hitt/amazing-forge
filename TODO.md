| 17 | **Bankable "+1 forward" (timing only) — OPEN, design deliberately or skip.** Raised 2026-07-15 (Galley/Aid discussion). The game already has BOTH forward-bonus channels: **Outstanding Success** (+1 to your own next roll) and **Aid Your Ally** (+2/+1 to the ally's next roll — already the deliberate prep action). The only genuinely open piece is **timing**: today the bonus burns on the *next* roll; banking would let it keep across scenes until spent (Starforged-momentum-lite) — that's what would make a galley pep-talk before the mission matter three scenes later. Banking is strictly stronger than next-roll (never wasted on a throwaway roll), so sim it before writing. **Prior art — Monster of the Week (Ian, 2026-07-15):** MotW splits "+1 forward" (next roll) from "hold" (spend later), and tames hold with three levers: **scope** (the granting move says what it's spent on), **expiry** (often dies with the situation), and **spend-rate** (spent one at a time, not stacked on one roll). For LCA the expiry lever looks like the winner: *"keeps until the current mission/Story beat ends"* bounds the bonus to the fiction that earned it — much safer than keeps-forever. **Stacking multiple banked +1s is ruled out** — it breaks the tuned +0/+1/+2 curve (+3/+4 rolls ≈92–97% hit, deletes the Miss economy the antagonist spine feeds on); if banking exists, cap at ONE held, never stacking with another temp bonus. | Low |
