## Worldbuilding Worksheet

The ten Worldbuilding questions with room to write. Fill it in at the table during the Worldbuilding step — one sheet builds one world. Answer in order: each question narrows the next, and by the time you reach the Threats the trouble almost names itself.

> **Printing tip:** use your browser's **Print** command (or Save as PDF) on this page. The site menus are hidden automatically, so you'll get just the worksheet. One sentence per answer is plenty — vivid beats complete, and "we'll find out" is a fine answer.

  <p class="af-title">LIGHTS, CAMERA, ACTION!</p>
  <p class="af-sub">Worldbuilding Worksheet</p>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">1.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Genre</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">What's the mood of our story?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">A feeling, not a setting. "Spooky &mdash; but the fun kind." What movie or show should this feel like?</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">2.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Setting</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">Where and when does it happen?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">One strong image with a twist. Not "a medieval kingdom" but one built inside a sunless canyon.</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">3.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Denizens</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">Who lives here?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">Two or three kinds, with a hint of how they relate. This is also what your heroes can be.</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">4.</span><span><span class="af-qname">Magic</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">How does it work, if at all?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">How common, and what it costs. "None at all" is a great answer. Sets what an Asset can be.</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">5.</span><span><span class="af-qname">Technology</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">What level are we at?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">Pick a level and add texture. "Laser blasters, but the ships run on duct tape and prayer."</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q af-break">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">6.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Leadership</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">Who's in charge?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">Name who, and hint how secure. A cracked power structure plays better than a stable one.</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">7.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Enforcement</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">How do they stay in charge?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">The muscle behind the power. Often the heroes' most frequent opposition &mdash; it becomes Challenges, not stat blocks.</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">8.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Threats</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">The two or three biggest dangers right now?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">Different scales and kinds &mdash; a creeping one, a sharp one, a hidden one. Make at least one concrete enough to tick a track (your Antagonist).</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">9.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Forbidden</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">One place, object, or action that's off-limits?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">Specific and tempting. "Nobody is allowed below the cloud line." A Story Arc waiting to happen.</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="af-q">
    <div class="af-qhead"><span class="af-qnum">10.</span><span><span class="af-qname">The Reputation</span> &mdash; <span class="af-qask">When people see a group like yours, what do they think?</span></span></div>
    <p class="af-hint">An outside opinion with an edge. "Reckless kids who don't know their place." Skip "famous heroes everyone loves."</p>
    <div class="af-fill"></div>
  </div>

  <p class="af-ref"><b>When you're done:</b> read the ten answers back in order &mdash; you'll usually feel a Story Arc forming in the overlap between the Threats, the Forbidden, and your Reputation. Leave the loose ends loose. Stuck on a question? Take the first decent answer, or just say "we'll find out."</p>
