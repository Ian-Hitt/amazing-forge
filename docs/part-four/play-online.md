# Play Online (Beta)

A lightweight digital companion for tracking a campaign — **no account, no sign-in.** Everything you make is saved right in your browser, and you can hand the whole campaign to other players with a single **share link**.

[**Open the Campaign Tracker &rarr;**](../play/campaign.html){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## What it does

One page gathers everything a campaign needs:

- **Characters** — the full Hero Sheet for everyone at the table: Concept, two Stats, three Assets (with Boons and the Broken flag), Readiness, and the Growth Track.
- **Story Arcs** — the Goal, the size (Episode / Movie / 2-player Movie), the **Story Arc Track** and **Antagonist Track** (with its reserved ★ climax box), the antagonist, and a numbered Milestone list. Checking a Milestone fills its track box automatically.
- **Challenges** — disposable scratch clocks for fights, chases, and tense scenes. Spin one up, fill it as you roll, and delete it (or "Clear finished") once it's done.

## How saving & sharing works

- **Saved in your browser.** Your campaigns live in this browser's local storage. They'll be here when you come back on the *same device and browser* — but they don't sync to the cloud and aren't tied to an account.
- **Share by link.** The **Share link** button copies a link that carries the *entire campaign* inside it. Send it to another player; when they open it, they get a full copy they can save on their own device.
- **A link is a snapshot.** Because there's no server, a shared link captures the campaign *at the moment you copied it.* If you keep playing, copy a fresh link to share the update. (Live, always-in-sync sharing is a possible future step — see below.)

!!! tip "Back up a campaign"
    Since campaigns live only in your browser, copy a **Share link** now and then and paste it somewhere safe (a notes app, a group chat). That link *is* a complete backup you can reopen any time.

## What's next

This is the quick, zero-infrastructure version. A future update could add real-time shared campaigns behind a short access code (so the whole table sees the same tracker update live) — but this Beta already covers solo prep, sharing a party, and running a session from your phone or laptop.
