# Chapter 6 — When You're Stuck

Even in a great session, the table sometimes goes quiet. A scene wraps up and nobody's sure what comes next, or a yes-or-no question stalls everyone. This short chapter is your toolkit for those moments — how to get unstuck and keep the movie rolling. It matters most in **Co-op play** (no Guide), where *you* decide what the world does next, but it helps any table.

### First, Name Your Next Milestone

The fastest fix is to remember what the heroes are actually trying to do right now. Look at your Hero Track — what's the next box? The next scene is almost always "a step toward that Milestone." Once you've named it, the obvious next move usually appears on its own.

If you're still stuck, reach for one of these.

### Do the Obvious Thing

Talk it out: in this movie, what would clearly happen next? Most of the time someone already knows. Run with it.

### Roll the Story Spark (d6)

Want a surprise? Roll one die and bend whatever you get toward your next Milestone:

| d6 | Story Spark |
|---|---|
| 1 | **A new face** arrives — an ally, an enemy, or someone you can't read yet. |
| 2 | **A discovery** — you find or notice something important. |
| 3 | **The threat closes in** — the antagonist or the world makes a move. |
| 4 | **A door opens** — a clue, a shortcut, or a lead toward the Milestone. |
| 5 | **A cost surfaces** — something you need is missing, broken, or guarded. |
| 6 | **A hard choice** — two good paths pull in opposite directions; pick one. |

### Ask the Dice (yes/no)

If it's really a yes-or-no question — "Is the gate guarded?" — roll **1d6:**

- **1** — No, and it's even worse than you feared.
- **2–3** — No.
- **4–5** — Yes.
- **6** — Yes, and there's more to it.

(If the answer seems likely, add +1 to the die; if it seems unlikely, subtract 1.)
