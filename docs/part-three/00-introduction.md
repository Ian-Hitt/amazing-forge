# PART THREE: BUILDING YOUR WORLD

*Building a world is its own kind of fun — a separate experience from playing the game, which is why it gets its own Part.*

Part One gave you the short version: ten questions, answered out loud in a few minutes, and you're playing. You never need more than that to start. Part Three is where you come when you want a richer, deeper setting — and it's **self-contained**, the one Part that's about *making* a world rather than *playing* in one.

You don't have to read it in order or all at once. Reach for it when an answer won't spark, when a Guide wants to prep a setting ahead of time, or when you simply want to spend an evening forging a world worth adventuring in.

Inside this Part:

- **Chapter 14 — The World Forge** takes the ten questions deeper: what a *good* answer to each looks like, ready-made **Starter Backdrops** for tables that don't want a blank page, how to run the Forge together or solo, and **idea tables** to roll on when you're stuck.
- **Chapter 15 — Genres** is the deep dive on the most important question of all: the six genres *Amazing Forge* is built to play, the playful-to-serious dial, and a **Genre Kit** for each to get you building fast.
- **Chapter 16 — Building a Cool Hero** is for when you want a character with a voice, a drive, and a flaw — not just four Assets and a number.
